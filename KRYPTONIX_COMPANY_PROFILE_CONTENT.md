# Recommended Design and Marketing Guidelines

## Brand presentation

- Use navy to communicate trust, gold for distinction, and white for clarity.
- Use one modern, easy-to-read font family throughout the deck.
- Keep each slide spacious and avoid long blocks of small text.
- Use professional images showing African businesses, teams, institutions, and customers where possible.
- Use real Kryptonix project images when permission is available.
- Maintain the same logo size, margins, heading style, icon style, and footer across the presentation.

## Tone of voice

- Confident, but never exaggerated
- Professional, but warm and approachable
- Focused on outcomes rather than complex technology
- Clear enough for a non-technical decision-maker
- Honest about current capabilities and experience

## Trust rules before publication

- Do not publish invented clients, testimonials, awards, certifications, partnerships, or statistics.
- Do not use another company's project screenshots as Kryptonix work.
- Obtain permission before naming a client or displaying its logo.
- Replace the Gmail address with an official domain email when available.
- Confirm the registered company name, physical address, founding year, registration details, and leadership information.
- Add a real team photograph or founder profile when approved.
- Proofread all text after placing it in Canva and keep the spelling **Kryptonix Technologies** consistent.
