# Kryptonix Technologies Brand Guidelines

**Version:** 1.0  
**Prepared for:** Marketing, sales, design, social media, partners, and external suppliers  
**Primary use:** Advertising posters, social media graphics, company profiles, proposals, presentations, brochures, banners, and sales materials

## 1. Brand Foundation

### Brand name

The official brand name is **Kryptonix Technologies**.

Use the full name on formal materials, first mentions, covers, proposals, advertisements, and official announcements. The shorter name **Kryptonix** may be used after the full name has already appeared.

Do not use inconsistent spellings such as “Kryptonics,” “Kryptonix Technology,” or “Kyptonix.”

### Brand purpose

Kryptonix Technologies helps organisations use technology to solve practical business challenges, improve operations, serve customers better, protect important information, and grow confidently.

### Brand positioning

Kryptonix Technologies is a trusted Kenyan technology partner providing practical, reliable, secure, and growth-focused solutions for startups, SMEs, institutions, NGOs, enterprises, and public-sector organisations.

### Core promise

> We make technology useful, understandable, and relevant to your goals.

### Primary tagline

**Technology Solutions That Move Businesses Forward**

### Supporting message

**Helping organisations work smarter, serve customers better, and grow with confidence.**

### Brand personality

Every Kryptonix communication should feel:

- **Trustworthy:** honest, responsible, and dependable
- **Professional:** organised, polished, and business-ready
- **Approachable:** clear, helpful, and easy to understand
- **Forward-looking:** modern, confident, and growth-oriented
- **Practical:** focused on real results instead of unnecessary complexity
- **Locally relevant:** aware of Kenyan and African business realities

## 2. Logo Guidelines

### Official logo asset

The website currently uses:

`public/Kryptonix logo.png`

This is the approved source asset currently available in the repository. A professionally prepared SVG, PDF, or EPS master should be created for large-format printing because the existing PNG may lose quality when enlarged.

### Preferred logo versions

Use these versions when they become available:

1. **Full-colour horizontal logo** — preferred for most materials
2. **White/reversed logo** — for dark navy or photographic backgrounds
3. **Single-colour navy logo** — for limited-colour documents
4. **Single-colour white logo** — for dark backgrounds
5. **Symbol-only logo** — only for avatars, favicons, watermarks, or very small spaces

Do not recolour the full logo using unrelated colours.

### Clear space

Leave empty space around the logo equal to at least the height of the letter **K** in the wordmark. No text, icon, photograph, border, or edge should enter this area.

For simple Canva use, keep at least:

- 40 px of clear space around the logo on a 1080 × 1080 design
- 60 px on a 1080 × 1350 design
- 20 mm on an A4 document cover

### Minimum size

- Digital full logo: no smaller than 160 px wide
- Print full logo: no smaller than 35 mm wide
- Symbol-only logo: no smaller than 24 px digitally or 8 mm in print

If the company name becomes difficult to read, use a larger logo.

### Correct placement

Preferred positions are:

- Top-left for posters, proposals, and information graphics
- Top-centre for formal covers
- Bottom-left or bottom-centre as a supporting signature

Use one logo per page or poster unless a second small footer logo is functionally necessary.

### Approved backgrounds

The logo may appear on:

- White
- Off-white
- Primary Navy
- A clean, low-detail section of a photograph with sufficient contrast

### Logo misuse

Never:

- Stretch, squash, rotate, skew, or crop the logo
- Change its proportions
- Add heavy shadows, bevels, outlines, or glow effects
- Place it on a busy image without a clean contrast area
- Put it inside an unapproved shape
- Separate or rearrange elements of the logo
- Recreate the wordmark using a standard font
- Use a low-resolution screenshot when the original asset is available

### Recommended colour balance

For most posters and sales materials, use approximately:

- **55–65%** white or off-white
- **20–30%** primary/secondary navy
- **8–12%** gold shades
- **5–10%** imagery and neutral supporting tones

Gold is an accent, not the dominant background. Large areas of bright gold can reduce the premium, trustworthy appearance of the brand.

### Approved colour combinations

Use:

- Primary Navy background + White text + Bright Gold call to action
- White background + Primary Navy heading + Dark Text body copy + Gold accents
- Off-white background + Primary Navy cards + White text
- Primary-to-Secondary Navy gradient + White text + Gold icons
- Light Gold highlight block + Primary Navy text

Avoid:

- Gold body text on white backgrounds
- Medium gray text on navy backgrounds
- Large rainbow palettes
- Neon colours unrelated to an official platform
- More than two gold shades in the same small composition
- Pure black as the primary brand background; use Primary Navy or Deep Midnight

## 5. Gradient System

### Primary navy gradient

Use for premium title panels, service banners, and calls to action:

`#081F5C → #102B72`

Recommended direction: 135 degrees, top-left to bottom-right.

### Deep navy gradient

Use for strong hero areas or dramatic final slides:

`#081F5C → #030D2A`

### Full brand gradient

Use only for thin lines, selected headline text, or controlled decorative accents:

`#081F5C → #102B72 → #D4A12E → #E0B347`

Do not apply the full four-colour gradient to long body text, large backgrounds, or the logo.

### Soft background glow

For digital designs, a subtle glow may use:

- Light Gold at 20–38% opacity
- Secondary Navy at 8–18% opacity
- Gold at 7–16% opacity

Glows must remain soft and should never reduce text readability.

## 6. Typography

### Primary typefaces

The website defines the following font system:

- **Poppins** — display headings and major campaign statements
- **Inter** — body text, descriptions, buttons, captions, and functional information

Both fonts are available in Canva and Google Fonts.

### Font hierarchy

#### Poster headline

- Font: Poppins
- Weight: Bold or SemiBold
- Recommended digital size: 64–96 px on a 1080 × 1350 poster
- Style: short, direct, sentence case or title case
- Maximum: 6–10 words where possible

#### Supporting headline

- Font: Poppins
- Weight: SemiBold
- Recommended size: 32–48 px

#### Body copy

- Font: Inter
- Weight: Regular or Medium
- Recommended size: 24–32 px on a 1080 × 1350 poster
- Line height: 1.4–1.6

#### Call-to-action text

- Font: Inter
- Weight: SemiBold or Bold
- Recommended size: 24–30 px

#### Eyebrow or category label

- Font: Inter
- Weight: Bold
- Use uppercase sparingly
- Add moderate letter spacing
- Use Gold or Primary Navy depending on the background

### Typography rules

- Use a maximum of two font families in one design.
- Use no more than three font weights on one poster.
- Left-align most marketing and sales content for easy scanning.
- Centre text only for covers, simple announcements, or one-message posters.
- Avoid long paragraphs on posters.
- Do not use decorative scripts, handwriting fonts, condensed display fonts, or novelty typefaces.
- Do not write complete paragraphs in uppercase.
- Use bold weight for emphasis instead of underlining.

### Example hierarchy

**EYEBROW:** WEBSITE DEVELOPMENT FOR GROWING BUSINESSES  
**Headline:** Turn Your Website Into a Business Asset  
**Support:** Fast, professional websites designed to build trust and generate enquiries.  
**CTA:** Request a Free Consultation

## 7. Layout and Composition

### Design principle

Every design should communicate one clear message first. Viewers should understand the offer, benefit, and next action within five seconds.

### Standard poster structure

1. Logo or brand signature
2. Small service/category label
3. Benefit-led headline
4. Short supporting sentence
5. Three benefits or proof points at most
6. One relevant image or visual
7. Clear call to action
8. Website, phone/WhatsApp, or QR code

### Grid and alignment

- Use consistent left and right margins.
- Align headings, body text, icons, and buttons to a shared grid.
- Use spacing deliberately; do not fill every empty area.
- Use one dominant visual rather than several competing photographs.
- Keep contact information together in one footer area.
- Keep the logo separate from the call to action.

### Recommended spacing for Canva

For a 1080 × 1350 portrait post:

- Outer safe margin: 70–90 px
- Gap between logo and headline: 50–80 px
- Gap between headline and supporting copy: 24–36 px
- Gap before call to action: 40–60 px
- Footer/contact safe area: at least 100 px tall

### Shape language

Use:

- Rounded rectangles with moderate corner radius
- Clean circular icon containers
- Thin gold dividers
- Soft shadows
- Subtle grid patterns
- Controlled glass-like white panels on light backgrounds

Avoid:

- Extreme rounded “bubble” styling
- Heavy 3D effects
- Sharp, aggressive zigzags
- Multiple overlapping decorative shapes
- Strong drop shadows around every element

## 8. Photography and Imagery

### Preferred photography

Choose images that show:

- African professionals and business owners
- Real teams collaborating
- Customers using services or digital tools
- Modern workplaces and growing organisations
- Retail, education, healthcare, hospitality, logistics, manufacturing, and public services
- Confidence, productivity, partnership, and progress

### Image style

- Bright, natural, professional lighting
- Authentic expressions and working environments
- Clean compositions with space for text
- Modern but believable technology
- Diverse people represented respectfully

### Images to avoid

- Masked “hackers” for every cybersecurity message
- Unrealistic glowing robots or science-fiction interfaces
- Generic server-room images on every technology poster
- Low-resolution or visibly stretched photos
- Images containing another company’s logo without permission
- Images implying partnerships, clients, or completed projects that cannot be verified
- Culturally irrelevant corporate imagery when a suitable African context is available

### Image treatment

For text over photography:

- Add a Primary Navy overlay at approximately 55–80% opacity.
- Place white text over the darkened area.
- Use Gold only for short highlights or icons.
- Check readability on a mobile phone before publishing.

### Project images

Use real project screenshots only when:

- Kryptonix completed or contributed to the work
- The client has approved public use
- Private data has been removed
- The screenshot is current and professionally presented

## 9. Iconography and Illustration

The website uses clean outline icons similar to the **Lucide** icon style.

### Icon rules

- Use simple line icons with consistent stroke width.
- Use one icon family throughout a design.
- Preferred icon colours: Primary Navy, Kryptonix Gold, Bright Gold, or White.
- Place icons inside simple circles or rounded squares when additional emphasis is needed.
- Do not mix flat icons, 3D icons, cartoons, and photographs without a clear system.
- Icons must support the message; they should not be decorative clutter.

## 10. Buttons and Calls to Action

### Primary call to action

- Background: Bright Gold `#E0B347`
- Text: Primary Navy `#081F5C`
- Shape: moderately rounded rectangle
- Text: short, specific, and action-oriented

Examples:

- Request a Quote
- Book a Consultation
- Call Us Today
- Chat on WhatsApp
- Start Your Project
- Get a Free Website Review

### Secondary call to action

- White background
- Primary Navy text
- Light Gray or low-opacity Navy border

Examples:

- View Our Work
- Learn More
- Explore Services

### CTA rules

- Use one primary CTA per poster.
- Do not present several equally strong actions.
- Keep the CTA close to the contact information.
- A QR code should have a clear label explaining its destination.
- Test every QR code before publication.

## 11. Voice and Messaging

### Tone

Write in a way that is:

- Clear and conversational
- Confident without exaggeration
- Professional without sounding distant
- Helpful rather than overly promotional
- Focused on the customer’s outcome

### Lead with benefits

Prefer:

> Sell online and manage orders more easily with an e-commerce website built around your business.

Avoid:

> We leverage cutting-edge full-stack frameworks and cloud-native architecture.

### Plain-language replacements

| Instead of | Say |
|---|---|
| Digital transformation | Improving how your organisation works using technology |
| Cloud migration | Moving your systems to a safer, more flexible online environment |
| Workflow automation | Reducing repetitive manual work |
| ERP implementation | Bringing key business operations into one organised system |
| Cybersecurity posture | How well your organisation is protected from digital risks |
| Scalable architecture | A solution that can grow with your business |
| API integration | Connecting your systems so they work together |

### Messaging formula

Use this structure for marketing copy:

**Problem or aspiration + practical solution + customer benefit + clear action**

Example:

> Losing customers because your website looks outdated? Kryptonix Technologies builds fast, professional websites that strengthen trust and generate enquiries. Request a free consultation today.

### Claims and trust

Never publish invented:

- Client numbers
- Project counts
- Awards
- Certifications
- Partnerships
- Testimonials
- Revenue or performance results
- “Number one,” “best,” or “leading” claims

All statistics, testimonials, partner logos, and case-study results must be documented and approved before use.

## 15. Print Specifications

For brochures, flyers, banners, and company profiles:

- Use CMYK colour mode where available.
- Export at 300 DPI for normal print materials.
- Add 3 mm bleed on each edge when required by the printer.
- Keep important content at least 5–8 mm inside the trim edge.
- Convert or embed fonts before sending final artwork.
- Export print-ready PDF using the printer’s requested standard.
- Request a proof before high-volume printing.
- For large banners, ask the printer for the correct scale and resolution.

Common formats:

- A4: 210 × 297 mm
- A5: 148 × 210 mm
- Square flyer: 210 × 210 mm
- Roll-up banner: commonly 850 × 2000 mm; confirm with supplier

## 16. Accessibility and Readability

- Use Primary Navy or Dark Text on white/off-white backgrounds.
- Use White on Primary Navy, Secondary Navy, or Deep Midnight.
- Do not use Gold for long text on white.
- Avoid placing text directly over high-detail images.
- Use at least 16 px equivalent body text digitally; social graphics normally need 24 px or larger.
- Do not communicate meaning through colour alone.
- Add captions to marketing videos.
- Add meaningful alt text when posting images online.
- Avoid flashing effects and excessive animation.
- Test designs on a mobile phone and in bright outdoor conditions.

## 19. Approval Workflow

Recommended process:

1. Marketing prepares the campaign objective and approved offer.
2. Copy is written and fact-checked.
3. Designer creates the first version using the Brand Kit.
4. Marketing reviews message, spelling, CTA, and platform format.
5. A technical or service owner verifies service claims.
6. Management approves prices, discounts, partnerships, testimonials, and major claims.
7. Final artwork is exported and links/QR codes are tested.
8. The approved source and export are stored in the shared folder.

No poster should be published directly from a first draft.
